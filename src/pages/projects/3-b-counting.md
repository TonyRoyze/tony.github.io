An accessible, command-driven desktop accounting application designed primarily for blind and low-vision users.

- **Guided command workflow:** Combines the speed of commands such as `new`, `summary`, and `find` with one-question-at-a-time forms and review-before-save safeguards.
- **Screen-reader first:** Uses semantic controls, predictable focus, concise announcements, and complete keyboard navigation.
- **Portable records:** Stores transactions in documented, human-readable **JSON Lines**, with CSV export and a clear audit trail.
- **Focused first release:** Prioritizes reliable transaction entry, search, summaries, categorization, export, and backup over complex bookkeeping features.
