An iOS assistive-reading prototype designed to help blind and low-vision users capture documents, verify recognized text, and listen to it quickly and accurately.

- **On-device reading pipeline:** Captures a document with the iPhone camera, checks image quality, performs OCR, and speaks the result.
- **AI-assisted verification:** Uses an imported on-device **Gemma** model through **LiteRT-LM** to verify and summarize recognized text.
- **Accessible controls:** Supports repeat, stop, adjustable speech speed, and an on-device reading history.
- **Wearable roadmap:** Integration with Meta Ray-Ban glasses and hands-free voice commands is planned.
