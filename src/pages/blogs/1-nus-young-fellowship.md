I recently completed the **NUS Young Fellowship Programme** at the National University of Singapore, representing the **Department of Statistics, University of Colombo**.

The fellowship brought together 112 young participants from more than 26 countries across five continents. One of the most rewarding parts of the experience was meeting, learning from, and building friendships with inspiring people from different cultures, disciplines, and walks of life.

Throughout the programme, I experienced many sides of NUS—from research and innovation to entrepreneurship, industry engagement, and its vibrant international community.

### Highlights

- Research and technical sessions led by NUS faculty.
- An industry visit to **Seagate Singapore**, where we learned about data-storage technology, chip design, and advances in lithography.
- An introduction to **GRIP**, the Graduate Research Innovation Programme.
- Inspiring conversations with NUS alumni founders about turning research into deep-tech ventures.
- Cross-cultural group projects that challenged us to exchange ideas and approach problems from new perspectives.

More than anything, the fellowship was about stepping into a new environment, listening to different perspectives, and creating meaningful connections with people from around the world. I am truly grateful for the learning, friendships, memories, and experiences I gained during my time at NUS. It is an experience I will carry with me for a long time. 🇸🇬
